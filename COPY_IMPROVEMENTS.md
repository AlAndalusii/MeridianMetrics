# ✅ Copy Improvements - Shorter & Clearer

## 🎯 Changes Made

Two key sections updated to be **shorter, punchier, and easier to read** while including both packaging AND waste.

---

## 1️⃣ Main Description (FAQ Section)

### BEFORE (Wordy - 57 words)
> "We design comprehensive environmental compliance systems—packaging compliance (EPR, PPT, PRN) and waste regulations (Duty of Care, Digital Tracking, Clinical Waste)—that eliminate penalties, exceed regulatory standards, and support your team."

### AFTER (Punchy - 24 words)
> "We manage your packaging (EPR, PPT, PRN) and waste compliance (Duty of Care, Digital Tracking, Clinical Waste). **No penalties. No gaps. No stress.**"

**Improvement:**
- ✅ 58% shorter
- ✅ More direct ("manage" vs "design systems")
- ✅ Punchy ending ("No penalties. No gaps. No stress.")
- ✅ Easier to scan and understand

---

## 2️⃣ Diagnostic Report Description

### BEFORE (Packaging only - 42 words)
> "Our free 3-minute assessment identifies compliance gaps across the key UK packaging regimes—PPT, EPR, PRN and WEEE records. In most UK businesses we review, 7 out of 10 have simple data or evidence gaps across schemes that could lead to avoidable costs and penalties."

### AFTER (Packaging + Waste - 39 words)
> "Our free 3-minute assessment identifies compliance gaps across UK packaging (PPT, EPR, PRN, WEEE) and waste regulations (Duty of Care, Digital Tracking, Simpler Recycling). In most businesses we review, **7 out of 10 have gaps** that could lead to penalties."

**Improvement:**
- ✅ Similar length (maintained character count)
- ✅ Now includes waste regulations
- ✅ Removed redundant words ("simple data or evidence", "avoidable costs and")
- ✅ More concise ("have gaps" vs "have simple data or evidence gaps")
- ✅ Clearer scope (packaging AND waste)
