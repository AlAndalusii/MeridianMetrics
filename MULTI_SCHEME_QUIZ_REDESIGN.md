# Multi-Scheme Compliance Quiz Redesign

Successfully redesigned the compliance assessment from a PPT-only focus to a comprehensive multi-scheme intelligence tool covering **EPR, PPT, and PRN/PERN** obligations. The quiz maintains the 3-minute completion time while dramatically expanding market intelligence capture and lead qualification capabilities.

## Scoring Logic

### Calculation Method:
- Questions 5-17 scored (13 questions)
- Each question worth 10 points
- Maximum possible: 130 points

### Scoring Rules:
1. **Business Profile (Q5-8):**
   - Clear answer = 8 points
   - "Not sure" = 0 points (gap indicator)

2. **Compliance Questions (Q9-17):**
   - ✓ Correct/Compliant = 10 points
   - Partial compliance = 5 points
   - ✗ Gap/Not sure = 0 points
   - N/A answers = 10 points (acceptable)

3. **Conditional Scoring:**
   - EPR-only: Skip PPT questions (Q12-14)
   - PPT-only: Skip EPR questions (Q9-11)
   - Score calculated only on applicable questions

### Score Interpretation:
- **90-100%:** Excellent compliance (managed service opportunity)
- **70-89%:** Good with minor gaps (comprehensive review)
- **50-69%:** Significant gaps (urgent audit needed)
- **Below 50%:** Critical compliance issues (immediate intervention)
