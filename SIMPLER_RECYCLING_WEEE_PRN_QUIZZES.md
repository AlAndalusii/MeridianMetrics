### QUIZ INTRODUCTION
**Title:** Are You Legally Selling Electronics in the UK? Find Out in 2 Minutes

**Description:**
WEEE regulations apply to anyone selling electrical products in the UK - from laptops to light bulbs.

If you manufacture, import, or sell electronics, you might need WEEE registration. Most online sellers don't know they're obligated.

This quiz tells you if you need to register.

Takes 2 minutes. Get instant answer.

## QUESTION 1: Product Type (Scope Test)
**What type of products do you sell?**

**Options:**
- A) Electrical/electronic products (anything with a plug, battery, or circuit)
- B) Non-electrical products only
- C) Mix of electrical and non-electrical products
- D) Not sure if my products count as "electrical"

**Why this matters:**
WEEE covers ALL electrical and electronic equipment (EEE) - computers, phones, TVs, fridges, light bulbs, electric toothbrushes, toys with batteries, etc.

If it has a plug, battery, or electronic circuit, it's covered.

**Scoring:**
- A = 10 points (definitely covered by WEEE)
- B = 0 points (WEEE doesn't apply)

- D = 8 points (need to check - probably covered)

- B) We import them from outside the UK (EU, China, USA, etc.)
- C) We buy from UK wholesalers and resell them
- D) We're a brand owner (products made for us with our brand name)

**Why this matters:**
You're a "producer" under WEEE if you:
- Manufacture in UK
- Import from abroad
- Sell under your own brand
- Sell via online marketplace from abroad

Producers MUST register for WEEE.

**Scoring:**
- A = 15 points (manufacturer = definitely producer)

- D = 15 points (brand owner = definitely producer)

**Why this matters:**
- **Under 5 tonnes/year:** Register as "small producer" directly with Environment Agency
- **5+ tonnes/year:** Must join a Producer Compliance Scheme (PCS) - costs more, more obligations

Most sellers underestimate their tonnage. 5 tonnes = less than you think.

**Scoring:**
- A = 8 points (small producer - still need to register)
- B = 12 points (large producer - must join PCS)

**Title:** Are You Obligated to Buy Packaging Recovery Notes? Find Out in 2 Minutes

**Description:**
PRNs (Packaging Recovery Notes) are evidence that packaging has been recycled. If you handle packaging in the UK, you might need to buy PRNs to prove recycling obligations are met.

Most businesses don't understand PRNs. Miss the deadline and you face criminal prosecution.

This quiz tells you if you need PRNs.

Takes 2 minutes. Get instant answer.

---

## QUESTION 1: Business Size (Turnover Threshold)
**What is your business's annual turnover?**

**Options:**
- A) Under £1 million
- B) £1 million - £2 million
- C) Over £2 million
- D) I don't know

**Why this matters:**
PRN obligations only apply to businesses with £1 million+ turnover handling 25+ tonnes of packaging. If you're under £1M, you're not obligated (for now).

But you still need to check packaging tonnage.

**Scoring:**
- A = 0 points (likely exempt - under turnover threshold)
- B = 5 points (check tonnage next)
- C = 8 points (check tonnage - likely obligated)
- D = 5 points (need to check)

## QUESTION 4: Packaging Activity Type

**Options:**

- B) We pack products into packaging (packer/filler)

**Why this matters:**
Different activities = different PRN obligations:
- **Manufacturers:** Lower obligation %

- **Sellers:** Lower obligation %

Importers pay the most PRN costs.

## QUESTION 6: Deadline Awareness
**When is the annual PRN compliance deadline?**

**Options:**
- A) 31 January (correct - each year)
- B) 31 March
- C) 31 December
- D) I didn't know there was a PRN deadline

**Why this matters:**
You must meet PRN obligations by **31 January each year** for the previous year's packaging.

Miss this deadline = criminal offence + financial penalties.

January 31, 2026 = deadline for 2025 packaging.

**Scoring:**
- A = 0 points (you know the deadline)
- B = 10 points (wrong deadline - will miss it)
- C = 10 points (wrong deadline - will miss it)
- D = 15 points (completely unaware - high risk)

## QUESTION 7: Current Registration Status
**Are you registered for packaging waste obligations?**

**Options:**
- A) Yes - registered with Environment Agency via Report Packaging Data (RPD)
- B) Yes - member of compliance scheme (Valpak, PackUK, etc.)
- C) No - I didn't know I needed to register
- D) No - I knew but haven't done it yet

**Why this matters:**
Before you can buy PRNs, you must:
1. Register with Environment Agency, OR
2. Join a compliance scheme that handles it for you

If you're not registered and need PRNs, you're accumulating non-compliance.

**Scoring:**
- A = 5 points (registered - but are you buying PRNs?)
- B = -5 points (compliance scheme handles PRNs - likely compliant)
- C = 20 points (URGENT - not registered = can't buy PRNs)
- D = 25 points (CRITICAL - knowingly non-compliant)

## QUESTION 8: PRN Procurement Knowledge
**Do you know how to buy PRNs?**

**Options:**
- A) Yes - we buy them from accredited reprocessors/exporters
- B) Sort of - we've heard of them but never bought them
- C) No - I have no idea how PRNs work
- D) What are PRNs exactly?

**Why this matters:**
PRN buying process:
1. Calculate your recycling obligation % for each material
2. Find accredited reprocessor or exporter
3. Purchase PRNs (£ per tonne, prices fluctuate)
4. Submit PRN evidence to Environment Agency by Jan 31

Get it wrong = non-compliant even if you tried.

**Scoring:**
- A = -5 points (know the process)
- B = 10 points (dangerous half-knowledge)
- C = 15 points (need expert help)
- D = 18 points (completely lost - urgent education needed)
